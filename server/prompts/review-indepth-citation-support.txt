=== CITATION SUPPORT ===
Also judge every [N] citation in the claims. For each cited claim, take the patient facts it gives that could come from a record (values with units, dates, drug names, results, diagnoses, statuses, stages) and check that its cited records state them, allowing different wording or date format; a claim citing several records is supported by them together. Leave out interpretation, advice, risks and general knowledge: they are not citation problems. A claim that gives no patient fact is supported only if its cited record states the guidance it gives. List in "unsupported" the NUMBERS of the claims with a fact their cited records do not state. Report an unsupported citation in "unsupported", not in "flag".

Add to the JSON object:
  "unsupported": [<claim numbers>]
